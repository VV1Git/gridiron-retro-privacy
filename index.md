# Gridiron Retro privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Gridiron Retro is a football game for iPhone. This page explains what the game stores and where.

## What stays on your phone

Your careers, settings and game progress are saved only on your phone. We don't receive them.

## Playing someone nearby

When you play a friend in the same room, the two phones connect directly over Wi-Fi. The game sends only
what the match needs: plays, positions and the score. Nothing goes to us. iOS asks your permission before
the game can look for other phones on your local network.

## Friends and online play

If you use the Friends feature, the game saves a small profile in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so your friends
can find you. The profile holds:

- the display name you choose
- your friend code
- your favourite team
- whether you're online or in a game, and when you were last seen

Anyone who has your friend code can add you and see this profile. Online matches go through Apple's
Game Center, which Apple runs under its own privacy policy. Its details are on
[apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

You can delete your profile at any time with DELETE MY PROFILE on the Friends screen. That removes your
profile, your friend list and any challenges you've sent.

## What the game doesn't do

- no ads
- no tracking, and no sharing or selling of data
- no analytics or third-party code
